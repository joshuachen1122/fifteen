How tuning works
Every hour the evaluation grid-searches min edge, volatility lookback and momentum weight on the oldest 70% of replayable trades, then scores the winner and the active parameters on the newest 30%. New parameters are applied only when they beat the active ones on those unseen trades, once there are enough trades, and at most once per cooldown period. Nothing here needs a button.
